Eventy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-26

Eventy processes webpage content or selected text only when you use the "Scan" action. The selected text or page text is sent to our Cloudflare Worker proxy and to OpenRouter to extract event details. Depending on your Settings, Eventy then either opens a prefilled Google Calendar page or creates events via the Google Calendar API after you authorize with Google.

What data we handle

  • No third-party trackers are embedded.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information. We do not maintain server-side user profiles.
  • Text and images you choose to scan are sent to our Cloudflare Worker and to OpenRouter solely to extract event fields (title, date/time, location, description, optional recurrence).
  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): If you provide your own OpenRouter API key, it is transmitted securely via our proxy to authenticate your requests. We do not store your API key on our servers.
  • When you enable "Use Google Calendar API (OAuth)", Eventy uses the minimal scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events to create calendar events you select. Eventy does not request broader calendar scopes.

OAuth and tokens

  • Authentication uses Chrome Identity. Access tokens are obtained from Google and cached by Chrome; Eventy does not store tokens on our servers.
  • Tokens are used only to call Google Calendar's REST API from the extension. They are not sent to our Cloudflare proxy.
  • You can revoke access at any time via Settings > Disconnect, or in your Google Account at Google Account > Security > Third-party access.

Storage and retention

  • Basic preferences (e.g., theme, whether API mode is enabled) are stored in your browser's chrome.storage. Event extraction results may be cached locally to improve UX. We do not store your data on our servers.
  • All network traffic is transmitted over HTTPS.
