AI-powered event extraction from webpages, emails, images, and posters - straight to your calendar.

Never manually copy event details again. Eventy uses AI to instantly extract dates, times, locations, and descriptions from virtually any source - webpages, emails, event flyers, concert posters, even screenshots. Select the events you want and add them to Google Calendar or export to iCalendar (.ics) for Outlook, Apple Calendar, and more.

QUICK START
1. Navigate to any page with event information.
2. Click the Eventy icon or press Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E on Mac).
3. Review detected events and select the ones you want.
4. Add to Google Calendar or download as .ics file.

SMART AI DETECTION
• Extracts dates, times, locations, and descriptions automatically.
• Works with webpages, emails, social media, images, posters, and flyers.
• Detects recurring events and preserves timezone information.
• Separates upcoming and past events for easy review.
• Page markers highlight detected events directly on the webpage.

MULTIPLE WAYS TO SCAN
• Click the extension icon to scan the full page.
• Right-click selected text to scan specific content.
• Right-click any image to scan posters, flyers, or screenshots.
• Paste custom text or upload photos directly in the extension.
• Use Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E on Mac) for quick access.

FLEXIBLE CALENDAR EXPORT
• One-click add to Google Calendar.
• Download iCalendar (.ics) files for Outlook, Apple Calendar, iCloud, and others.
• Batch add multiple events at once.

CUSTOMIZATION
• Light, Dark, or Auto theme (follows system preference).
• 12-hour or 24-hour time format display.
• Settings sync across your Chrome browsers.

POWER USER OPTIONS
• Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK) for unlimited scans.
• Choose from 100+ AI models via OpenRouter.
• Fine-tune extraction with your preferred provider.

PRIVACY FIRST
• Scans only when you activate it - never runs in the background.
• No tracking, no analytics, no data collection.
• Your event data stays in your browser until you export it.
